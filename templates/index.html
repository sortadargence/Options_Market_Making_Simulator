Reading the averageFills are equally likely on bid and ask, independent of model error. A pricing mismatch can help buys and hurt sells, or the reverse; these effects can cancel in the combined average. The model does not simulate adverse selection. With matched pricing and sufficiently fine hedging, average P&L per contract is expected near the quoted half spread.
